There is a particular kind of calm in Utrecht before the cafés fill up. The water of the Oudegracht sits still, the wharf cellars are shuttered, and the whole city feels like it belongs to the handful of people already out walking.

## Start early

Aim to be by the canal before eight. The light is low and gold, and the only sounds are gulls and the occasional bicycle. This is the best hour for photographs, with no crowds crossing your frame.

## Down to the water

Take the stairs down to the wharf level. Walking right beside the water, below street height, is something few other cities offer. The old cellars that once stored wine and goods now hold quiet terraces, still empty at this hour.

## End with coffee

By nine, the first places open their doors. Pick a spot facing the water, order a strong coffee, and watch the city slowly wake up around you. It is a gentle way to start any day in Utrecht.
