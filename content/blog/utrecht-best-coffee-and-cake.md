Utrecht's coffee scene is quietly excellent. The student population keeps a lot of small, opinionated cafés in business, and the canal-front terraces give you a view that costs a fortune in Amsterdam. This guide pins nine places: pure third-wave espresso bars where the barista will tell you the farm name, hundred-year-old bakeries with stroopwafels you watch being made, and a few hybrid spots where you can eat well enough to skip lunch.

## Where to start — by mood

If you only have time for one coffee, make it [Blackbird Coffee](#place-blackbird) for the espresso or [Village Coffee & Music](#place-village-coffee) for the filter pour. Both are walkable from the Dom Tower.

If you're meeting someone for breakfast, [Anne&Max](#place-anne-fleur) on the Twijnstraat will keep them happy whatever they order — and the bakery [Jordy's](#place-jordys) is two doors down, so you can grab a loaf on the way out.

## A bakery crawl, 25 minutes

Start at [Bakkerij Bond](#place-the-village) on the Choorstraat — get a koffie verkeerd and watch the stroopwafels go onto the griddle. Walk south along the Oudegracht (it's the photographed two-level canal you've seen in every Utrecht photo) and you'll pass [Broodje Mario](#place-broodje-mario) on your right at number 117 — grab a salami focaccia for the road. Cross the next bridge and you're on the Twijnstraat, where [Jordy's](#place-jordys) closes the loop with a chocolate-spelt sourdough.

## For long sits

[GROUND](#place-ground) is the one to settle into. They don't rush you, the marble counter is roomy, and the wifi is fast. [Blossom Coffee Bar](#place-blossom) is the opposite — tiny, two stools, you order and stand — but the espresso is unmatched if you only have ten minutes.

[Lavinia Good Food](#place-lavinia) for brunch, especially Sunday: the plaza is one of Utrecht's quietest, and you can stretch a long pot of tea into two hours.

## A note on opening hours

Dutch cafés keep different hours than you might expect. Most coffee bars don't open before 09:00 and many close at 17:00 — Utrecht is a "coffee in the morning, beer in the afternoon" city, not a New York one. Check Google before walking across town.
